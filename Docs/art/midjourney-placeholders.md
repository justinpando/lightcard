# Midjourney Placeholder Prompts — v2 Catalog (49 cards)

## Workflow

1. Generate with the prompts below (square, `--ar 1:1`).
2. Remove the background (remove.bg, Photoshop, or Midjourney's web editor)
   and export a PNG **with alpha**.
3. Save it as `Assets/Art/CardPlaceholders/<exact card name>.png`
   (e.g. `Assets/Art/CardPlaceholders/Siege Knight.png`), overwriting the
   procedural placeholder if one exists.
4. Run **LightCard → Sync Card Assets From Catalog**. A PNG in that folder
   always wins: it replaces procedural art *and* the old 2021 sketches, and the
   deck builder, hand, and board tokens all update automatically. On the board
   the figure stands directly on the field, on an owner-colored base strip.

## House style

Append to every card prompt for a consistent, cuttable set. Every subject is a
**single figure or object, no scenery, no ground, no companions** — light
effects that are part of the subject itself (a glow, sparks, petals it emits)
are fine; anything behind or beneath it is not:

> `loose ink and watercolor illustration, symbolic dreamlike imagery from the
> collective unconscious, single subject isolated on a plain white background,
> full figure in frame, no scenery, no ground, no cast shadow, card game art
> --ar 1:1 --no text, letters, frame, border, background scenery, landscape`

Archetype palettes (from the design sheet): **Garden** verdant greens with rose
accents · **Expedition** warm golds and sunlit ochre · **Atelier** deep
workshop blues with brass · (later: Heart embers-red, Ocean violet, Tower
iron-black).
